About the data

The games use the Social Acceptance of Sustainable Data Centres in Ireland survey from Maynooth University: 200 people, 104 questions. We only use totals per answer and cross-tabulations - no individual response ever leaves the original file, and groups smaller than 3 people are hidden.

A survey tells us what these 200 people said. It isn't the whole of Ireland, and an opinion isn't a fact - so every game keeps survey answers, measured facts and your own guesses visibly apart.
