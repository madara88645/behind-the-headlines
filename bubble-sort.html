    The three bubbles

    Data
    Something that was measured and has a source you can check. ("The CSO measured…")
    Opinion
    A judgement about what is good, bad, fair or what should happen. It can't be proven true or false - but how many people hold it is itself data.
    Assumption
    A claim about how the world is that could be checked, stated without evidence. It might turn out true, false or half-true.

    How to play

    • Pick how sure you are: Hunch (1 pt), Fairly sure (2 pts) or Certain (3 pts). Right wins the points, wrong loses them.
    • Drop the card: drag it into a bubble, tap the card then a bubble, or press a bubble.
    • After an assumption lands, guess whether it is true before you see the check - then compare with the survey.
    • Keyboard: 1 2 3 drop the card, H F C set your bet, T F N answer true / false / not sure, Enter goes on.

    Survey numbers come from the Maynooth University survey of 200 people in Ireland. Facts come with a source link you can open.
