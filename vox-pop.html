Vox Pop

Ask the street. Guess the town. Then meet the 200.

Three radio stories. Can a few interviews tell you what a town thinks?

  1. Ask residents around town.
  2. Guess a percentage for your headline.
  3. Compare it with the real survey.

Opinion Built on a survey of people in Ireland (Maynooth University survey). Fictional town and residents; answers sampled from survey totals.

  • DataSourced facts at teal markers.
  • OpinionInterviews and survey views.
